# Five profiles, one professional network, zero real people

Everyone below is fictional. The platform is fictional. The photographs are
gradients with a silhouette on top, because the lesson is in the **metadata** and
the **context**, not in a face — and because we do not put real people's faces in
a public repository to be scored as "probably fake".

The organisation they orbit is **Tagus Logística** (`tagus-logistica.example`),
the same one being phished in `../*.eml`. That is not a coincidence. Block 1 feeds
Block 2.

Each card is what you would see on the profile page, captured by a colleague who
has since gone on holiday and left you their scoring sheet
(`profiles-colleague.csv`). They were in a hurry. It shows.
